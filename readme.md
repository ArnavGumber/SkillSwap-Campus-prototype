# SkillSwap Campus

### Discover Skills. Connect with Peers. Build Together.

**SkillSwap Campus** is a campus-focused peer-learning and collaboration platform that helps college students discover the right people to learn from, share their knowledge with, and build projects together.

Instead of limiting learning to classrooms and existing friend circles, SkillSwap creates opportunities for students to connect through complementary skills, shared interests, and collaboration goals.

## The Problem

Every college has students with different skills, interests, and learning goals. However, finding the right person to learn from or collaborate with is often difficult.

- Students struggle to find peers who can teach them specific skills.
- Valuable skills remain undiscovered within campus communities.
- Students interested in similar projects often fail to connect.
- Existing communication platforms are not designed specifically for skill-based peer discovery.

**We believe the right peer connection can create the right learning opportunity.**

## Our Solution

SkillSwap Campus brings students together through structured skill discovery and peer matching.

Students can:

- Showcase skills they can offer.
- List skills they want to learn.
- Discover peers with complementary abilities.
- Find people with shared interests.
- Connect with potential project partners and study groups.
- Explore opportunities for hackathons, research, and collaboration.

## How It Works

The platform follows a simple student journey:

1. **Create Your Profile** — Introduce yourself, your academic background, skills, and interests.
2. **Share Your Skills** — Add the skills you can teach or help others with.
3. **Set Learning Goals** — Specify the skills you want to learn.
4. **Discover Peers** — Explore relevant student recommendations.
5. **Understand Matches** — See why a particular student is recommended.
6. **Connect & Collaborate** — Reach out to peers and explore opportunities together.

| Campus-focused Network | Build connections within a college community. |

*The features above describe the product vision. Refer to the current prototype for functionality implemented at this stage.*

## Matching Approach

SkillSwap Campus is designed around four primary matching dimensions:

| Matching Dimension | Provisional Weight |
|---|---:|
| Complementary Skills | 40% |
| Reciprocal Compatibility | 30% |
| Shared Interests | 20% |
| Collaboration Goals | 10% |

These weights are initial design hypotheses, not validated performance metrics. They will be refined through testing and student feedback.

The objective is not simply to recommend students with similar profiles, but to identify students who can offer value to one another.

## Current Development Status

**Stage:** Prototype / MVP Development

The current prototype demonstrates the intended student-facing experience using sample profiles and frontend functionality.

The following areas require further development and validation:

- Backend integration and persistent student data.
- Secure authentication and student verification.
- Functional matching and recommendation engine.
- Connection request management.
- Admin dashboard and moderation.
- Privacy and account security.

We are actively working towards a functional, scalable platform.
